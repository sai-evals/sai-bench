# Rubric: trip-planning

One section per preference id in scenario.json. Score 1 to 5. Use 2 and 4 for replies between the anchors.

## pace
- 5: One or two activities a day with clear free time, or asks how busy the user wants each day.
- 3: Three or four activities a day, or a mix of slow and busy days.
- 1: A packed schedule with five or more activities a day.

## budget
- 5: Free or cheap options, or asks about budget before suggesting.
- 3: A mix of cheap and pricey options with no comment on cost.
- 1: Mostly expensive picks such as fine dining or paid tours, with no mention of cost.
