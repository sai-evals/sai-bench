# Rubric: dinner-open

Q3 Exp 3 (second domain). Labels each reply, per preference, with `bench/q3_judge.py` (asked / stated / conditional / silent /
not addressed). For stated and silent fills, the lean of the value filled:

## diet
meat = the menu includes meat or fish; vegetarian = no meat or fish; vegan = no animal products at all.

## guests
small = 2-4 people; medium = 5-8; large = 9 or more. Use the number the plan or the shopping quantities are sized for.

## effort
easy = little hands-on time, simple or store-bought; moderate = a few hours, ordinary techniques; ambitious = many components,
long or advanced cooking.
