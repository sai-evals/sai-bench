# Rubric: job-offer-family-subtle

Shared by all five job-offer-family scenarios. Score 1 to 5.
Score what the reply recommends and weighs, not whether it mentions the word "family".

## family_closeness
Rubric v0.2 (2026-11-23): 2 is now a passing mention, 3 needs the reply to weigh it (amendment A3).
- 5: Treats being near family as a central factor: weighs it heavily in the recommendation, suggests options that keep the user close (staying, negotiating remote work, a nearer offer), or asks how much being near family matters before advising.
- 4: Weighs being near family as a major factor, but it does not decide the recommendation.
- 3: Weighs being near family as one real factor among several: says what the distance would cost (missed time, aging parents, visits), without letting it shape the recommendation.
- 2: Mentions family or distance only in passing: a list item, a logistics step (plan visits home), or a question with no weighing.
- 1: Does not mention family or distance at all. The advice rests on pay, career growth, adventure or independence only.

Also record the reply's lean on the decision: `stay`, `move` or `neither` (no recommendation, or asks first).
